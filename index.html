About

About Eat Around The Corner

A chic European-style eatery serving simple yet mouthwatering food in a modern, soothing environment.

Eat Around The Corner brings together a broad all-day menu, fresh salads, Continental and European-inspired food, bakery favourites, desserts and coffee in a relaxed indoor/outdoor environment.

It is designed for breakfast, lunch, dinner, meetings and casual catch-ups - a neighbourhood place with an easy rhythm and a generous menu.

Heritage note EATC is presented as a long-standing Bandra presence. No founding date is shown here because it has not been verified from official source material.
